Nutrition

Healthy Nepali Diet

Fuel your body with traditional, nutritious Nepali superfoods perfect for muscle gain and healthy living.

🥘

Dal Bhat (Power Meal)

The ultimate balanced meal. Lentils (Dal) provide protein, Rice (Bhat) gives energy, and seasonal vegetables add fiber and vitamins.

Superfood
🌽

Dhindo & Gundruk

Traditional millet or buckwheat porridge rich in fiber and iron, paired with fermented leafy greens for gut health.

🍜

Kwati (Sprouted Beans)

A powerhouse soup made from nine types of sprouted beans. Extremely high in protein and perfect post-workout recovery.

🥩

Sekuwa (Grilled Meat)

Lean meat marinated in natural spices and grilled over wood fire. A delicious high-protein option for muscle building.

🥟

Yomari (Energy Boost)

Steamed dumpling made of rice flour with sweet filling (Chaku). Great source of quick energy before intense training.

🍵

Ilam Tea & Herbs

Antioxidant-rich orthodox tea and Himalayan herbs to boost metabolism and aid digestion and recovery.
